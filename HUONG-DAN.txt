1. Cai Node.js 24 hoac moi hon. Trong thu muc website, chay: npm start
2. Mo http://127.0.0.1:8002 (khong dung server tinh cong 8000/8001).
   Giao dien cua hang: vao admin > Giao dien cua hang (/admin-giao-dien.html).
   Sua logo/ten cua hang, banner, cau chuyen, khoi dang ky va chan trang.
   Nhom 4 cam ket cua hang: sua tieu de va mo ta tung o tren trang chu;
   bieu tuong va thu tu 4 o giu nguyen. Bam Luu giao dien de ap dung.
   Nhom Lien he dau trang: sua so/chu Hotline, chu va dia chi Email,
   ten va lien ket Gioi thieu; dong bo tren 5 trang co thanh dieu huong.
   So/email dau trang chinh rieng voi chan trang. Lan dau lay so/email
   chan trang dang luu, khong mat noi dung cu.
   Chon anh PNG/JPEG/WebP tu may (toi da 8 MB, tu thu gon) hoac nhap URL anh.
   Bam Luu giao dien de luu anh va chu vao SQLite tren may chu, sau do tai
   lai trang cua hang. Logo/chan trang dong bo tren 7 trang cua hang.
   Menu chinh/dropdown van chinh trong muc Menu, khong bi ghi de.
   Neu co nguoi luu truoc, ban nhap duoc giu va bao loi; Tai lai de lay ban moi.
   Form dang ky trang chu luu ten, so Zalo, email (khong bat buoc) tren may chu.
   Vao admin > Khach dang ky (/admin-khach-dang-ky.html) de xem; bam Lam moi
   de lay dang ky moi, co phan trang 25 luot/trang. Cua hang tu lien he khach,
   chua tu gui tin Zalo. Neu gui loi, form giu thong tin de khach thu lai.
   Dang ky luu trong SQLite; danh sach chi quan tri duoc xem.
3. Vao /admin-login.html de dang nhap. Tai khoan: Manh Trinh.
   Mat khau do ban dat trong Environment tren Render: ADMIN_PASSWORD.
   Trang admin yeu cau dang nhap ca khi chay npm start tren may cuc bo.
   Mo link admin truc tiep se chuyen sang dang nhap; phien keo dai 8 gio.
   Nut Dang xuat co tren moi trang admin. Sau deploy can dang nhap lai.
   Lan dau, terminal van hien mat khau quan tri du phong. Luu o noi an toan.
   Muon doi mat khau: dat bien moi truong ADMIN_PASSWORD (it nhat 12 ky tu)
   roi khoi dong lai may chu. Khong ghi mat khau vao ma nguon.
4. San pham va voucher trong admin hien la ban nhap tren trinh duyet.
   Trong danh sach san pham, bam mui ten len/xuong de doi thu tu.
   Thu tu tu dong luu vao ban nhap; bam Dang san pham ra web de ap dung
   cho trang chu va danh sach gao (ke ca khi loc tim kiem/danh muc).
   Trang San pham co nut Dang san pham ra web: dang ban nhap san pham
   va giu voucher dang ban tren may chu. Can xac nhan truoc khi thay the;
   cac san pham bi go duoc liet ke trong hop xac nhan. Loi khong mat ban nhap.
   Sau khi sua, vao Du lieu cua hang (lien ket tren trang San pham,
   hoac /admin-du-lieu.html) > Dang du lieu len may chu.
   May chu kiem tra gia, voucher va ton kho khi khach gui don.
   Tren thiet bi admin moi, Tai du lieu ve trinh chinh sua truoc khi chinh.
   Khi xem cua hang, du lieu may chu duoc nap ve cache rieng cho khach,
   khong thay the ban nhap cua admin. Khi quay lai tab cua hang, san pham
   va thu tu da dang duoc tai lai tu may chu, khong can tai lai ca trang.
   Du lieu cu o cong 8001/file HTML: mo admin-du-lieu.html tren dia chi cu,
   bam Xuat ban nhap san pham & voucher. Tren cong 8002, mo Du lieu cua hang va nhap
   tep nay de dang du lieu. Khong tu dong di chuyen du lieu cua ban.
5. Khach bam Dat hang ngay, nhap ten, dien thoai, dia chi va gui don.
   Don moi hien trong admin khi bam Lam moi don hang.
   Muc Don hang tren menu admin hien so do dem don chua xem, kiem tra moi
   30 giay khi tab dang mo va khi quay lai tab. Tai danh sach don thanh cong
   se danh dau da xem tren may chu; don den sau moc tai van giu thong bao.
   Nut Dang xuat nam duoi cung menu admin, khong che noi dung chinh sua.
   Tren dien thoai (rong toi da 650 px), menu admin mac dinh dong. Bam nut
   menu de mo/dong; chon lien ket, bam ngoai menu hoac Escape de dong lai.
   Chua thanh toan online, chua tu tinh phi giao hang, chua tru/giu ton kho.
   Admin tu xac nhan don, cap nhat ton va dang du lieu moi len may chu.
6. Co so du lieu: data\store.sqlite. Sao luu bang cong cu SQLite backup
   hoac dung may chu truoc khi sao chep ca thu muc data.
   Don luu thong tin ca nhan: chi cap quyen truy cap cho nguoi quan tri;
   khong dua file data, file sao luu hay mat khau len Git/repository cong khai.
7. Len hosting: chon VPS/hosting ho tro Node.js 24 va o dia ben vung cho SQLite.
   Dat HOST=0.0.0.0, PORT theo hosting, PUBLIC_ORIGIN=https://ten-mien-cua-ban.
   Sau khi gan ten mien va bat HTTPS, kiem tra /robots.txt va /sitemap.xml,
   khai bao sitemap trong Google Search Console. Trang blog duoc phep index,
   bai viet va danh muc blog duoc luu tren may chu trong data\store.sqlite,
   trang cong khai chi hien bai da dang. Bai blog cu trong localStorage se duoc
   chuyen len may chu khi mo trang quan tri bai viet sau khi cap nhat.
   Khong dua trang admin, gio hang hay dat hang vao sitemap.
   Mui gio het han voucher mac dinh Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh (co the doi bang TZ).
   Bat HTTPS qua reverse proxy, gioi han kich thuoc request 25 MB va backup dinh ky.
   DATABASE_PATH co the tro toi o dia ben vung; khong dung o dia tam/serverless.
   Neu nhieu may chu cung ghi du lieu, can chuyen sang PostgreSQL/MySQL.
8. Kiem tra: npm test (dung co so du lieu tam, khong thay doi don that).
9. Menu: vao admin > Menu (/admin-menu.html). Them/sua/xoa menu chinh,
   them muc dropdown voi ten va lien ket rieng; dung mui ten len/xuong de sap xep.
   Bam Luu menu de dang truc tiep len may chu, khong can cong cu Du lieu cua hang.
   Menu dung chung cho trang chu, danh muc, chi tiet gao va hai trang bai viet.
   Nut Luu menu nam trong thanh thao tac ghim cuoi man hinh khi cuon.
   Giao dien chia ro khoi danh muc va menu chinh; moi the hien ten menu,
   so muc dropdown va nut doi thu tu/xoa. Cham vang bao thay doi chua luu.
   Menu co muc con: bam ten de mo dropdown, roi chon danh muc ben trong.
   Lien ket chinh nam o muc Xem tat ca trong dropdown.
   Menu khong co muc con van mo lien ket truc tiep.
   Dropdown mot cap, toi da 12 menu chinh va 20 muc con moi menu.
   Khoi Danh muc gao ngon ben trai cung duoc quan ly tai day: sua ten,
   lien ket chinh, them/xoa/sap xep toi da 200 muc lien ket rieng.
   Khi nang cap, danh sach ban dau lay tu san pham may chu hien co.
   Sau do danh muc duoc quan ly rieng, khong tu doi khi sua/them san pham.
   Dung dia chi may chu cong 8002; ban HTML mo truc tiep chi hien menu co san.
10. Danh muc san pham: vao admin > Danh muc san pham (/admin-danh-muc.html).
    Bam Tao danh muc san pham, nhap ten (vi du Gao lai), tich chon cac san pham
    thuoc danh muc roi bam Luu danh muc. Mot san pham co the thuoc nhieu danh muc.
    San pham duoc chon la ban da dang len may chu; neu thieu san pham moi,
    dang tu Du lieu cua hang truoc roi Tai lai danh muc.
    Trang gao.html?category=gao-lai chi hien san pham thuoc danh muc Gao lai.
    Tim kiem tren trang nay chi tim trong danh muc hien tai.
    Trang Tat ca gao va trang tung danh muc co hang nut danh muc tren san pham.
    Nut dang chon to xanh; chuyen danh muc giu tu khoa tim kiem neu co.
    Tao danh muc moi them lien ket o cuoi dropdown va hien day du trong Menu.
    Quan ly Menu la nguon duy nhat cua dropdown: ten, lien ket, thu tu trung khop.
    Xoa muc trong Menu khong xoa danh muc va khong tu them lai khi tai trang.
    Nang cap tu ban cu giu cac muc danh muc truoc day bi chen ngam vao dropdown.
    Trong Menu, dung Chon danh muc san pham de tu dien ten va lien ket.
    Xoa danh muc khong xoa san pham. Doi duong dan/xoa danh muc: sua lien ket
    da gan vao menu tuy chinh neu co. Du lieu danh muc luu truc tiep may chu,
    khong phai ban nhap localStorage va khong nam trong tep xuat san pham/voucher.
    Luu xong quay ve danh sach gon: moi danh muc co Sua/Xoa va len/xuong.
    Sua mo trinh chinh sua rieng; Huy bo ban nhap, khong thay doi danh muc da luu.
    Xoa va sap xep luu truc tiep len may chu sau thao tac (Xoa co xac nhan).
11. Danh muc bai viet: admin > Danh muc bai viet (/admin-danh-muc-bai-viet.html).
    Tao ten va duong dan, luu xong hien danh sach Sua/Xoa/len/xuong.
    Trong Bai viet, chon danh muc tu danh sach khi soan/sua bai roi luu bai.
    Cac chuyen muc cu duoc giu lai; doi ten danh muc khong lam mat nhom bai.
    Xoa danh muc khong xoa bai, bai chuyen sang Chua phan loai.
    Trang bai-viet-danh-sach.html?category=meo-nau-com chi hien bai da dang
    thuoc danh muc do. Co cac nut danh muc tren trang danh sach bai viet.
    Menu co lua chon danh muc bai viet de tu dien ten va lien ket.
    Bai viet va danh muc bai viet VAN luu localStorage tren trinh duyet/origin
    hien tai; chua dong bo may chu hay SEO cong khai. Dung cung cong 8002.
    Lien ket menu luu may chu nhung bai/danh muc local khong tu sang thiet bi khac.
    Trong tao/sua danh muc bai viet, co anh, tim kiem va tich chon bai truc tiep.
    Moi bai thuoc mot danh muc: chon bai o nhom khac se chuyen sang nhom hien tai.
    Bo chon dua bai ve Chua phan loai. Tat ca chi ap dung sau khi Luu danh muc.
